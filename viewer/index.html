Format, controls and interpretation

Use the standard Azure Kinect order: pelvis is joint 0, left hip/knee/ankle/foot are 18–21, and right hip/knee/ankle/foot are 22–25. CSV headers, frame-number columns, missing cells and nonfinite values are rejected with a line number. A UTF-8 BOM and trailing blank lines are allowed.

Drag to orbit in 3D, scroll to zoom, or choose a fixed projection. Arrow keys step frames and Space toggles playback when focus is outside a form control. The scene uses equal spatial scale on all axes. Centering on the pelvis removes translation for display only; it also hides the trail.

Time is nominal: frame index ÷ FPS. Playback uses real elapsed time and may skip displayed frames on a slow computer; every frame remains accessible with the slider. These coordinates are raw observations. Camera −Y is a display direction, not a calibrated gravity axis. No ground plane, contact, CoM, forces or fall labels are inferred.

Millimeters and 30 FPS are defaults for SmartFallMM, not verified metadata. Confirm the export conventions before physical interpretation. The adjacent-frame displacement is a diagnostic, not an error or fall classifier.

SmartFallMM data: SmartFall Group, Texas State University. No uploads, external scripts or network dependencies.
